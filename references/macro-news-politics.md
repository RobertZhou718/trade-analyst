# Macro, policy, politics and news: how to read the drivers

## Macro transmission cheat-sheet

| Driver | Most sensitive |
|---|---|
| Fed / rate expectations, 10y & real yields | unprofitable tech, small caps, REITs, crypto, gold |
| Bank of Canada, CAD | Canadian banks, REITs, utilities, telecoms; weak CAD helps USD earners listed on TSX |
| CPI/PCE, jobs (NFP; Canada LFS) | growth multiples, cyclicals |
| USD (DXY) | multinationals, commodities |
| Oil (WTI, WCS), gold, copper | TSX energy & materials, airlines |
| VIX, credit spreads, liquidity/ETF flows | high beta, crypto |

## Political & policy factors to check

tariffs & retaliation (US–Canada–Mexico/CUSMA matters a lot for TSX goods producers), export controls (chips), fiscal policy / shutdowns (delay data), regulation & antitrust, crypto rules (SEC/CFTC, stablecoin/market-structure bills, ETF approvals), elections & central-bank leadership, geopolitics/sanctions (oil, defense, safe havens), sector programs (defense, healthcare, pipelines, subsidies). For each that applies: *what happened (date) → mechanism → direction for this asset → how priced-in*. Describe policy effects neutrally, not whether a policy is good.

## News triage

(impact high→low): earnings/guidance → M&A, contracts, approvals, rulings → analyst changes (clusters matter) → insider buying, buybacks, dilution → management changes → opinion pieces. Tag each `+ / - / 0` with one line why.

## Sentiment & positioning

When available: short interest % float, options skew/unusual activity, consensus vs price; crypto funding/OI/ETF flows. One-sided extremes = caution flag, not a signal.
